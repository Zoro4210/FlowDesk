How to Use FlowDesk

Designed to remove all friction from habit tracking.

1

Add the Widget

Long-press your Android home screen and place the Medium (4x2) or Small (2x1) FlowDesk widget for instant access.

2

Log Without Opening

Tap the + button directly on your home screen widget to log your habit for today. The action is fully native and happens instantly.

3

View the Heatmap

When you want the full picture, open the app to see a detailed 16-week grid. Switch between Study, Tasks Done, and Custom habits.

4

Zero Friction

Enjoy a natively matched dark theme layout with absolutely no splash screens or waiting times. Stay in the zone.
